Privacy Policy

At Denim District, protecting your privacy is important. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you shop with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, order details, and payment information when you place an order or contact customer support.

How We Use Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To communicate with you about products, support, and shipping updates.
  • To improve our website experience, services, and customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand browsing patterns, remember preferences, and improve site performance.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers needed to fulfill orders, process payments, and support our operations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at help@denimdistrict.com.
